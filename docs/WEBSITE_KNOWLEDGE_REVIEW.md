## Published knowledge scope

The reviewed seed contains public facts about school identity, grades, CBSE and ICSE,
teaching methodology, assessment, facilities, meals, transport, day care, admissions,
fee-information policy, enquiry privacy, contact details and the JGI Group.

Marketing slogans, duplicated navigation, image captions and claims that do not help
answer a parent question were omitted.

## Deliberately unpublished or qualified information

### Office and campus hours

The dedicated Contact page states Monday through Saturday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
Repeated website footers state Monday through Saturday, 9:30 AM to 5:00 PM.
Because the official pages conflict, the chatbot must escalate questions about hours
until the school confirms the correct value.

### Fees

The website does not publish exact amounts. The chatbot may explain what the fee
structure can include and how to request it, but it must not generate any amount.

### Seats

The website says seats are limited but publishes no live counts. Questions about a
specific grade, board or session must be escalated to admissions.

### Eligibility

Published age ranges are indicative. Exact date-of-birth cutoffs and transfer
eligibility must be confirmed for the applicable session.

### Transport

General transport availability is public. Route coverage, pickup times, availability
and pricing for a particular address require confirmation.

## Maintenance recommendation

Review session-specific claims before every admissions cycle. Recheck grade availability,
the growth roadmap, contact details, fees policy, transport, eligibility and operating
hours whenever the website changes.
